How it goes
  1. Every piece the machine could find is already outlined. Switch to Adjust and drag its nodes onto the real edge — that is most of the work gone.
  2. For anything it missed: click your way round the piece, corner by corner. Straight runs take two clicks; drag instead if you would rather sketch a curve freehand.
  3. Let go of the mouse whenever you like — the outline stays open until you close it yourself.
  4. Click the first node again to close it, or press Enter.
  5. Outline just inside the printed cut line — the cutter pulls in another hair, so that line never reaches the finished piece.
  6. If the sheet is a scan or a photograph rather than a PDF at true size, set its scale first — measure something on it whose real length you know, or every size here is a guess.
  7. Export, and send me the file. The mask PNG cuts the artwork out of the sheet; the cut SVG is the same outlines at true size, for the laser.
